The Audiopheliac

Install on iPhone, iPad, or the Samsung tablet. Same address on all of them — the one printed in the black window on the PC.

It looks like http://192.168.1.XXX:8099

iPhone / iPad — Safari only. Not Chrome. Not Grok.

  1. On the PC, double-click GO.cmd and leave it open.
  2. On the phone open Safari.
  3. Type the address from the black window and go.
  4. Tap Share (box with an arrow).
  5. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  6. Open the Audiopheliac icon. That is the remote.

Samsung tablet — Chrome. Same address → menu → Add to Home screen.

Open the remote
