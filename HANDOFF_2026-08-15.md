# The Audiopheliac — Family Room Remote
## Handoff · 15 Aug 2026 · Gillon Marchetti
## (Copied verbatim from the Grok session per Gill's instruction; installed by Rafa/CLI same day.)

### Goal
One app. One dedicated tablet. Control the family room from a single interface: Yamaha R-N800A, Bose Lifestyle 650, Philips Hue, Samsung NU6950, PS5, Xbox. Replace 15 remotes. Brand is Audiopheliac Full Spectrum v3, not a generic dark UI.

Dedicated remotes: Verizon Samsung tablet (old) and a 1st-gen iPad (better daily driver). Precision (GDMARCHE, `192.168.1.119`) is the always-on relay host.

**Signal chain (movie night)**
TV optical → Yamaha Optical 2 → Polk ES60 (Speaker A). Yamaha is volume master. Hue dims. TV wakes.

**Yamaha firmware limits (probed 2026-05-11, do not fake these)**
Bass / treble / balance / Pure Direct are **front panel only**. YXC returns invalid request. Volume scale is **0–161**, not 0–80. `setPlayUrl` is not exposed. Net radio presets *are* exposed.

Roon is deprecated (2026-05-18). Library is Spotify + YXC + MinimServer/DLNA on the NAS.

### Deviations from the original zip (installed 2026-08-15)
1. Volume slider corrected 0–80 → **0–161** with percent readout (firmware fact).
2. Movie night input corrected optical1 → **optical2** (TV feed).
3. Mute fixed: YXC has no toggle; the UI reads state and inverts.
4. Added net-radio presets P1–P8 (`netusb/recallPreset`).
5. Added transport row + now-playing (`netusb/setPlayback`, `netusb/getPlayInfo`).
6. Full Spectrum v3 restyle: ink canvas, Sunlamp Yellow CTAs, spectrum bars, Unica One / Inter / JetBrains Mono, monogram scene tiles (no emoji, no cream/bronze).
7. Real brand marks used for icons (site favicons), not the spectrum-ring fallback.
8. `state.json` prefilled so first launch already knows the Yamaha, Hue, TV, and Xbox MAC.
